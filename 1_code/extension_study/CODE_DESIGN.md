# Code design: extension study

**15 October 2026.** Code for `../08_EXTENSION_DESIGN.md`. Every module below maps to a decision there; the
panel report behind each is named in brackets.

---

## 1. Principles that the code enforces

1. **One source of truth for data.** Raw-data readers and the audit live in `../code/pcgecg/`. This package imports
   them; it does not re-read raw files its own way.
2. **IDs travel with data.** Every array saved carries its dataset, subject, record, and site; every join asserts
   matching IDs first. [lesson: shuffled-label ablation]
3. **Groups, not records.** Paired data split by (dataset, subject); CirCor split by child = connected components of
   Patient ID and Additional ID; zero overlap asserted in every fold. [A, D, F]
4. **The CirCor firewall.** Training and selection code cannot import a CirCor path: `config.py` exposes CirCor paths
   only to `infer_circor.py`, `springer/`, and the R1 adapter, and a test fails if any other module references them.
   [D, F]
5. **Stable filters only.** Second-order sections with a pole-radius assertion. [lesson: June detector]
6. **Selection on beat identity,** not on waveform metrics. [C]
7. **No hand-typed numbers.** Statistics write a results JSON and a LaTeX macro file. [D, F]
8. **Hashes everywhere.** Data manifest, fold files, checkpoints, builder, and scorer are hashed into the
   pre-registration and the ledger. [D, E, F]

## 6. R-peak detection (built 15 Oct 2026)

The demo app's false R-peaks came from four faults (Panel H, `../panel_review/H_rpeak_review/REPORT.md`): a band-pass
designed in numerator-denominator form at 8 kHz (pole radius 1.0088, so the band-pass path failed and a
75th-percentile envelope threshold ran on every window), a search over zero padding, snapping to the largest raw
deflection within 40 ms (spikes win), and invented beats at 80 bpm with no flag. The study code takes none of these
paths.

**Detector.** `extstudy/robust_beats.py` is Panel H's detector, copied unchanged; its SHA-256 (09bb9d7f...) was frozen
before it saw paired data, and a test checks it. Second-order-section filters with a pole assertion; candidates on
QRS slope energy at least 120 ms apart; an expected heart rate from autocorrelation (40 to 220 bpm, half-lag check); an
emission score that penalises narrow spikes, T waves taller than the QRS, wide deflections, and poor template
correlation; a two-pass Viterbi decoder over log-RR transitions (sinus, premature, long, missed beat). The beat
position is the centroid of 5 to 25 Hz energy within plus or minus min(60 ms, 0.15 RR), iterated twice; it does not
depend on polarity, so it cannot jump between R and S.

**Teacher rule (`qrs.teacher_beats`).** A detected beat is kept when XQRS or GQRS finds it within 50 ms, after
removing each wfdb detector's own record offset (GQRS marks the QRS onset, 20 to 50 ms before the centroid). The
wfdb input is scaled by the median 2 s block maximum and clipped at 3 QRS amplitudes. Beats found by XQRS and GQRS
alone are not added, but the cycle that holds one carries no labels; so does a cycle holding a rejected candidate
with at least half the local beat level's QRS energy that one wfdb detector confirms. A kept beat is good when its
template correlation is at least 0.8. No labels: the two cycles next to a beat that is not good; neighbourhoods
where under 80% of the beats within plus or minus 5 s are good; RR above 1.5 times the local median (08 design);
NaN stretches.

**What the tests changed.**

| Test | First version | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spikes, teacher | beats agreed by XQRS and GQRS added back | 7 false beats per minute at 40 bpm: both wfdb detectors accept spikes together | additions dropped |
| One large artefact | XQRS on the scaled trace | XQRS missed 347 of the next 375 beats (F1 0.12) | input clipped at 3 QRS amplitudes (F1 0.997) |
| Agreement per 60 s window as a mask | XQRS-GQRS F1 0.90 and detector-XQRS F1 0.95 | masked spike windows and every window above 180 bpm (GQRS loses beats there) where the detector was right | reported only; replaced by local confirmation |
| 20 s of electrode noise | local confirmation alone | 85% of the noise labelled at 50 bpm: the rhythm model placed beats, and XQRS and GQRS fired 2 to 3 times per second | template correlation at least 0.8 (noise median 0.5; real ECG: median record has no beat below 0.8) |
| Bigeminy (first relabelling, then simulated) | gap and correlation rules only | in bigeminy the decoder keeps the regular normal-to-normal chain; every RR looks regular, so no mask fires, and each labelled cycle hides a premature beat (30 usable HFrEF records with at least 25% such beats, label coverage 1.0) | the cycle holding a beat found by both wfdb detectors carries no labels; simulated wide premature beats escape XQRS (0% found, GQRS 100%), so a rejected candidate with QRS energy plus one wfdb detection also masks its cycle |
| Stress F1 at least 0.98 per signal | Panel H's proposal | its own detector met it on 66 of 72 signals at 40 to 200 bpm (lowest 0.94); the reported 0.988 was a mean over rates | acceptance as a mean per condition; per-signal values printed |

**Acceptance (`tests/test_qrs.py`).** Every filter stable; frozen hash; detector mean F1 at least 0.98 per condition at
40 to 200 bpm (tall T and combined at 220 bpm stay reported: 0.92 and 0.87); teacher F1 inside the label mask 1.00
in every condition (0.9995 with premature beats), precision at least 0.997, coverage 0.75 to 1.00; no labels inside
pauses, NaN stretches, or electrode noise; no labelled cycle that hides a premature beat in bigeminy at 60, 75, and
90 bpm; centroid SD at most 6 ms on biphasic QRS, where the largest deflection jumps between R and S.

**Shared beat decoder (`extstudy/decoder.py`, `tests/test_decoder.py`).** One decoder for T and W (Panel H, decision 2):
the frozen detector's expected-RR estimate and two-pass Viterbi, with emission from amplitude alone. Two changes came
from simulated event traces. First, the emission scale: 4 log2(amplitude / local beat level) + 2 instead of 2 log2 + 1
(same zero point, 0.71 of the beat level); at +1 per beat, one restart (-12) dropped whole stretches whenever a third
of their beats were missing (11 true beats lost after a 3 s pause). Second, T's input is its beat head minus its S2
head, floored at 0: an S2 sequence is as regular as the beat sequence, and with S1 missing in a stretch the decoder
followed the S2 chain (F1 0.39 to 0.45 on the raw head; 0.99 to 1.00 after the subtraction). Suite of 36 simulated
traces at 40 to 220 bpm with S2 residue, spurious bumps, missing and premature beats: median F1 0.992 against the
beats that carry a bump, 10th percentile 0.978; on simulated ECG (W's read-out) precision 0.97 to 1.00.

**Relabel step (`extstudy/relabel.py`).** One lead per dataset (SensSmartTech II, BSSLAB channel 4; the next lead only
when the first fails the ECG check: at least 5 beats, label coverage at least 0.5, at least 90% of detected beats
confirmed). Synchronisation statistics on the labelled beats, per record under 120 s and per 60 s window on longer
records (08 design); the negative control is rerun with the new beats. Outputs in `derived/beats_v2/`.

**Labels (`extstudy/labels.py`, built 15 Oct 2026).** S1 and S2 targets are PCG-envelope maxima inside R-locked
windows (08 design 2.6): S1 within law_S1(dataset, HR) plus or minus 40 ms, S2 within law_S2(dataset, HR) plus or
minus 80 ms, with the laws (intercept and slope per dataset) fitted by median regression on training subjects only.
At 80 bpm S1 follows the QRS reference by 38 to 89 ms and S2 by 327 to 343 ms; S1 shortens with rate in EPHNOGRAM
(-0.31 ms/bpm) and SensSmartTech (-0.68), S2 by 1.3 to 2.1 ms/bpm. A beat is valid when its cycle is labelled and
regular and both sounds pass: off the window edge, at least 2 record noise units above the cycle floor, at least a
quarter of the record's typical height, at least as tall as anything else in the cycle, and S1 not before the QRS.
Four rules changed after checks: a per-cycle z-score failed 87% of EPHNOGRAM S2 above 150 bpm (S1 and S2 fill a third
of a short cycle), so heights are measured in record noise units; one slope shared across datasets came out near 0,
so each dataset has its own; doubtful marks in the 200-beat check sat on small bumps in cycles holding a larger event,
so the dominance rule was added; 65 HFrEF and 7 training-a records had S1 marks at or before R, so a record with a
median S1 lag under 10 ms carries no labels. Valid training beats: 122,929 (25.0 h); 21,231 at 100 to 125 bpm, 8,894 at 125 to 150, 2,956 at 150 or more. EPHNOGRAM exercise S2 is the weakest part (49% of clean beats
valid): faint S2 against motion noise.

**Labels v2 (17 to 18 Oct 2026).** The 200-beat check of v1 failed (164/200 ok; mostly faint or competing S2). v2 raises the height floor to 4 record-noise units and adds lag consistency (each mark within 40 ms of the running median of its sound's lag over 11 neighbouring candidates). Training beats: 89,660 (BSSLAB 94% of v1, training-a 88%, EPHNOGRAM 50%, FOSTER 99%, HFrEF 87%, SensSmart 65%); laws within 5 ms of v1 below 110 bpm. The blinded re-check (fresh records, 40 controls) failed: 141/200 ok, 160/200 if split or flat-topped S1 counts as ok; controls 35/40 wrong. The failures left: faint S2 (S2 height separates them, AUC 0.88) and split or flat-topped S1 (no recorded feature separates them). Outputs in `derived/labels_v2` and `derived/corpus/paired_v2`; v1 kept unchanged.

**Labels v3 and the label gate (18 Oct 2026).** v3 adds three S2 rules: height at least 6 record-noise units, at least 0.3 of the S1 height in the same cycle, and no other peak in the S2 window more than 40 ms away taller than 0.6 of it. The gate, logged before any comparison, follows the field's practice (Springer et al. 2016; Liu et al. 2016): marks are judged against the hand-corrected heart-sound annotations of PhysioNet/CinC 2016 training-a (Springer's segmentation checked by one person for obvious errors), correct when on the annotated complex of the same sound plus or minus 20 ms. v3: S1 99.98% [99.95, 100], S2 99.88% [99.76, 99.97] (5,805 beats, 240 records); beats removed by v2 and v3 had 5 to 16 times the error rate of those kept. The reference detects gross errors only: the heart-rate law alone scores 97.4% for S2. Claude's visual verdicts did not track the reference (27 'wrong', 1 confirmed; kappa 0.06 to 0.09), so the visual check is reported, not used as the gate: v3 187/200 ok, controls rejected by v3 50% wrong. `refcheck.py` and `tests/test_refcheck.py` hold the check; `notebooks/build_train.py` applies the gate.

**Run 2 and Experiment 0 (18 Oct 2026).** `infer.py` runs the frozen T and W on a recording as in training (30 s windows, seed averages, shared decoder); `cycles.py` exports R1 cycles: W's R-peaks through arm B's construction (verbatim), T's S1 events and S2 head weighted by a Gaussian around the paired law's interval (SD 40 ms) and 50% acoustic onsets. On paired test records, T and W find 97 to 100% of labelled S1 onsets within 50 ms (true-ECG ceiling 97 to 100%). `build_run2.py` patches the locked Run 1 notebook by cell name (no generator; arm E rescored only as a reproduction control); `merge_run2.py` merges with Run 1 and runs the frozen stats.py. `corrupt.py` and `build_exp0.py` build Experiment 0's 60 corrupted copies of arm C (missed beats, spurious beats, jitter) on the murmur-present recordings, with arm C as the reproduction control; `exp0_analysis.py` applies the pre-registered read-off.

**Training pack (`extstudy/corpus.py`).** `derived/corpus/paired_v1/`: band-passed PCG at 1 kHz (int16, 412 MB), per
record spectrogram medians, records table, events, and a SHA-256 manifest that `corpus.load()` checks on Colab.
2,908 records, 57.5 h; valid beats 122,929 train, 23,124 validation, 24,371 test.

## Implemented layout (note added 29 October 2026, after an independent code review)

The layout above was the plan of 15 October 2026. The code as run differs in names only: `infer_circor.py` became `infer.py` (label-free inference of the frozen networks) and `cycles.py` (cycle construction at 4 kHz); `metrics.py` became `refcheck.py` and `label_check.py` (label gates) with the beat metrics inside `decoder.py` and `train.py`; `ledger.py` was not written, the notebooks append to `runs/ledger.csv` directly; `r1_adapter.py` became the notebook builders in `notebooks/`. The firewall test (`tests/test_firewall.py`) scans every module except `config.py`, `splits.py`, `stats.py`, and the `springer/` package for CirCor references; `infer.py` and `cycles.py` are scanned and clean. The decoder's gap mask (`decoder.py`, `mask500`) is computed but not applied on CirCor: a missed beat gives one long cycle, as in arm B.

## Modules added for the two pre-registered follow-up experiments (29 October 2026)

`noise.py` (Experiment 1, `Next Study - Beat-Accurate Synthetic ECG/EXP1_PREREG.md`): in-band interference confined to
20 to 500 Hz, three kinds (band-limited Gaussian, bursts, ESC-50 crying, breathing and coughing), mixed at a stated
in-band SNR with a per-recording generator seeded from the condition seed and the CRC-32 of the recording name;
the notebook `Experiment1_InBandNoise.ipynb` (builder `build_exp1.py`, over the locked Run 1 notebook with Run 2's
patch set) corrupts the 610 murmur-present recordings at test time only, builds segmentation-only and both-stage
arms, and classifies with Run 1's machinery; `exp1_analysis.py` computes H1 and the estimation contrasts locally.

`labels_acoustic.py` and the kind `A` additions to `model.py`, `losses.py`, `train.py` and `infer.py` (Experiment 2,
`EXP2_PREREG.md`): S1 and S2 labels for the paired pack derived from the phonocardiogram alone (Springer segmenter at
arm S's primary setting, envelope maxima inside its S1 and S2 states, the validity rules of labels v3 with the
S1-state onset as beat reference; no ECG sample enters any mark); kind `A` is the T architecture with S1 and S2 heads
and no R head, trained by `Extension_Train_AC.ipynb` (builder `build_train_ac.py`) with T's crops, augmentation,
schedule, seeds and selection rule, and scored on CirCor by `Extension_Run3.ipynb` (builder `build_run3.py`, Run 2's
patch set with A in place of T and W); `exp2_analysis.py` computes H1 (T minus T_ac, two-sided) and the estimation
contrasts. T and W inference is unchanged by the kind-A additions; the Run 2 arms are not rescored. The firewall
test scans both new modules; neither refers to CirCor.
